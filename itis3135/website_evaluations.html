CRAP Website Evaluations

Apple

  • Contrast: Apple uses strong contrast with clean white backgrounds, dark text, and bold product images that immediately draw attention.
  • Repetition: The site consistently uses the same fonts, colors, button styles, and layouts throughout every page, creating a cohesive experience.
  • Alignment: Content is carefully aligned using grids and consistent spacing, making information easy to scan and visually appealing.
  • Proximity: Related information is grouped together logically. Product descriptions, images, and calls to action appear near one another, improving usability.

Apple demonstrates excellent application of the CRAP principles. Its minimalist design enhances readability and provides users with a polished and intuitive browsing experience.

Arngren.net

  • Contrast: The website uses numerous colors, fonts, and text sizes without purpose, making important information difficult to distinguish.
  • Repetition: There is little consistency in typography, image placement, or styling. Elements appear random and unrelated.
  • Alignment: Objects are scattered across the page without a clear grid or structure, creating a cluttered appearance.
  • Proximity: Related items are not grouped together effectively. The lack of organization makes navigation confusing and overwhelming.

Arngren.net is often cited as an example of poor web design because it violates many of the CRAP principles. The excessive clutter and lack of structure negatively affect usability.
