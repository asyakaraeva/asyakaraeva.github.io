Shop Hours

Adorable Koala is open throughout the week for customers who want to browse our comfort products, create care packages, or pick up online orders. Our quieter weekday mornings are ideal for customers who prefer a peaceful shopping experience.

Online orders may be placed at any time. Orders submitted after business hours are prepared on the next open day. Personalized gift boxes may require one additional business day to complete.
