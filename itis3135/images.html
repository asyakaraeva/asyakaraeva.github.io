Everything Images

This page demonstrates how images can be manipulated for use on the web. It explores image formats, proper resizing techniques, CSS scaling, image compression, and the importance of maintaining correct proportions. Two different photographs are used: one downloaded from Pexels and one personal photograph taken specifically for this assignment.

Demonstrating Image Manipulation with a Gourmet Cheese Board Photo from Pexels

I selected this image because it contains many fine details, including fresh figs, blueberries, walnuts, soft cheese, and a glass of rosé wine. The photograph was taken by Nati and downloaded from Pexels. Their photography features elegant food styling and natural lighting. The original image is a JPG with a high resolution, making it an excellent example for demonstrating proper image editing and scaling.
