About me and my baking hobby

Througout my entire life I've been surrounded by baking. My grandmother, aunts, and mom all love to bake

From constantly helping my family bake to now baking whenever I get the chance, it's always been a hobby of mine.

  • Favorite dessert: Chocolate cake
  • Favorite cookie: Chocolate chip
  • Favorite bread: Banana bread

Baking has become a way for me to connect with family traditions and bring in new recipies.

What is Baking?

Baking is one of my favorite hobbies because it allows me to be creative while making delicious treats.

I enjoy experimenting with recipes and learning new decorating techniques.

The things I bake most often are:

  • Cakes
  • Cookies
  • Muffins
  • Banana Bread

When Do I Bake?

I usually bake on weekends when I have extra time (or when i'm stressed and need something to take my mind off things).

Holiday seasons are my favorite time for baking special treats. Especially testing new recipies for my family.

I have my favorites throughout the year as well!
Season Favorite Recipe
Spring Lemon Cake
Summer Berry Muffins

Winter Holiday Cookies

Where Do I Bake?

Most of my baking takes place in my home kitchen.

I enjoy baking for family gatherings and celebrations!

I don't always bake in my home kitchen, sometimes I bake with family or friends.

  • Home Kitchen
  • Family Gatherings
  • Holiday Events

How Do I Bake?

Baking requires patience, preparation, and attention to detail.

Following recipes carefully helps ensure good results.

The most basic outline for baking is:

  1. Gather ingredients
  2. Preheat oven
  3. Mix ingredients
  4. Bake and cool

Why I Love Baking

Baking helps me relax and reduce stress. Though it does also cause me stress when recipies get difficult..

It allows me to express creativity through recipes and decorations.

I enjoy sharing homemade treats with friends and family.
